# Recap – Week 1

> Window: 14 Sep – 20 Sep

## Week 1 Frenzy: Olave & Watson Shine, Allen & Henry Duel, and Close Calls Galore!

**Moments That Mattered**
- Naveb edges out Warkha in a WR showdown featuring Olave and Jefferson lighting up the scoreboard.
- Mavbenson’s Watson and Montgomery combine for a dominant performance to steamroll RedCordial.
- Dell2620’s Giants duo Likely and Dart spark a strong win over NickDagan despite Gibbs’ monster day.
- Mheineger’s Allen and St. Brown outduel JamesHanlin’s Henry and Swift in a tight QB vs RB battle.
- Wastingtime02 pulls off a mild upset over leftovername, powered by Kenneth Walker’s huge RB day.

### naveb (1-0) 137.2 – 126.3 warkha (0-1)
**Star Performers:** 🎯 Chris Olave (NO WR – 28.2 pts), 🎯 Justin Jefferson (MIN WR – 31.2 pts)
In a thrilling WR shootout, naveb's Chris Olave and Jaxon Smith-Njigba combined for over 54 points to outpace warkha's Justin Jefferson, who put up a massive 31.2 points himself. Although Javonte Williams chipped in a solid 24.2, it wasn’t quite enough to hold off the dynamic duo. Naveb’s depth at receiver gave them the edge, turning a tight contest into a narrow victory.

### jacross2000 (0-1) 94.02 – 122.96 TheMianimal (1-0)
**Star Performers:** 🏃 Bijan Robinson (ATL RB – 31.3 pts), 🎯 Zay Flowers (BAL WR – 26.0 pts)
TheMianimal's Bijan Robinson was a force to be reckoned with, racking up 31.3 points and leading the charge past jacross2000. Despite Zay Flowers’ impressive 26 points, jacross2000 couldn’t overcome TheMianimal’s balanced attack, including Nico Collins’ 21.2 points. Jacross2000’s Breece Hall showed promise but the overall team output fell short on this opening weekend.

### mavbenson (1-0) 148.22 – 108.76 RedCordial (0-1)
**Star Performers:** 🎯 Christian Watson (GB WR – 32.7 pts), 🏃 David Montgomery (HOU RB – 28.9 pts)
Mavbenson’s powerhouse week was fueled by Christian Watson’s explosive 32.7 points and David Montgomery’s strong 28.9 showing. Their combined firepower overwhelmed RedCordial, whose best came from Parker Washington and the JAX defense but still couldn’t keep pace. This dominant performance sets mavbenson up as a team to watch.

### Dell2620 (1-0) 136.8 – 99.82 NickDagan (0-1)
**Star Performers:** 🧲 Isaiah Likely (NYG TE – 27.8 pts), 🧠 Jaxson Dart (NYG QB – 26.6 pts)
Dell2620’s Giants tandem of Isaiah Likely and Jaxson Dart combined for over 54 points to power past NickDagan. Despite Jahmyr Gibbs’ monster 33.6 points, NickDagan’s supporting cast couldn’t keep up. Dell2620’s strong QB-TE combo proved a winning formula in this one-sided affair.

### Wastingtime02 (0-1) 104.46 – 117.66 leftovername (1-0)
**Star Performers:** 🏃 Kenneth Walker (KC RB – 34.1 pts), 🏃 Ashton Jeanty (LV RB – 32.7 pts)
Leftovername’s Ashton Jeanty and Jonathan Taylor put up big numbers but it was Wastingtime02’s Kenneth Walker who stole the show with a huge 34.1 points. Jayden Daniels added a steady 17.66 to help Wastingtime02 pull off a mild upset. This was a tight RB battle that went down to the wire.

### Mheineger (1-0) 120.96 – 115.3 JamesHanlin (0-1)
**Star Performers:** 🧠 Josh Allen (BUF QB – 35.66 pts), 🏃 Derrick Henry (BAL RB – 35.3 pts)
Mheineger and JamesHanlin treated fans to a fantasy heavyweight bout, with Josh Allen’s 35.66 points narrowly outpacing Derrick Henry’s 35.3. Amon-Ra St. Brown’s 28.7 points gave Mheineger the extra push needed to edge out JamesHanlin, despite D’Andre Swift’s 32.4 points. This nail-biter was a perfect Week 1 thriller.
