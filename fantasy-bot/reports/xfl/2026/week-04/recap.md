# Recap – Week 4

> Window: 05 Oct – 11 Oct

## Week 4 Frenzy: Naveb’s McMillan Madness and Jacross2000’s WR Show Steal the Spotlight

**Moments That Mattered**
- Tetairoa McMillan explodes for a massive 45.2 points, powering naveb to a dominant 4-0 start.
- CeeDee Lamb’s 41.3 points nearly single-handedly put Dell2620 over the top in a nail-biter.
- Kenneth Walker’s 30.9 points spark Wastingtime02 to an upset over Mheineger despite Kyren Williams’ monster day.
- Drake Maye’s QB breakout and Alvin Kamara’s steady production edge NickDagan past TheMianimal’s high-scoring effort.
- Jonathan Taylor and Lamar Jackson combine for a solid outing but can’t overcome Javonte Williams’ 31.3 points for warkha.

### naveb (4-0) 155.92 – 86.32 mavbenson (3-1)
**Star Performers:** 🎯 Tetairoa McMillan (CAR WR – 45.2 pts), 🧲 Brock Bowers (LV TE – 20.6 pts)
Naveb continued their perfect run with a crushing 155.92 to 86.32 win over mavbenson, thanks largely to Tetairoa McMillan’s breakout 45.2-point performance. McMillan was unstoppable, turning every target into fantasy gold. Brock Bowers also chipped in a strong 20.6 points, giving naveb a balanced attack. Meanwhile, mavbenson’s Christian McCaffrey managed 16 points but was well contained overall. Spencer Shrader’s 16 points from kicking couldn't keep pace with the onslaught. Naveb’s dominance on both skill positions sealed a statement victory and kept their unbeaten streak alive.

### jacross2000 (1-3) 138.68 – 84.56 RedCordial (2-2)
**Star Performers:** 🎯 Puka Nacua (LAR WR – 27.7 pts), 🎯 Zay Flowers (BAL WR – 25.8 pts)
Jacross2000 snapped their losing skid with a big 138.68 to 84.56 win over RedCordial, riding a potent WR duo. Puka Nacua and Zay Flowers combined for over 50 points, terrorising defences with big plays and consistent targets. RedCordial’s James Cook provided a decent 16.3 points, but the supporting cast fell flat. Cam Little’s 14 points from the boot was not enough to keep pace. Jacross2000’s WR-heavy approach paid off handsomely, turning the tide in a matchup where explosive receiving yards made all the difference.

### Dell2620 (2-2) 107.8 – 96.62 JamesHanlin (0-4)
**Star Performers:** 🎯 CeeDee Lamb (DAL WR – 41.3 pts), 🧠 Patrick Mahomes (KC QB – 17.0 pts)
Dell2620 finally got over the hump with a gritty 107.8 to 96.62 victory over struggling JamesHanlin. CeeDee Lamb put on a show with a massive 41.3 points, nearly carrying the team solo. Patrick Mahomes added a steady 17 points to keep the scoreboard ticking. JamesHanlin’s Malik Nabers impressed with 23.2 points, but Derrick Henry’s 15.9 wasn’t enough to close the gap. Dell2620’s star power at WR and QB proved just enough to break their losing streak and keep hopes alive.

### Wastingtime02 (1-3) 139.68 – 155.12 Mheineger (3-1)
**Star Performers:** 🏃 Kenneth Walker (KC RB – 30.9 pts), 🎯 Tee Higgins (CIN WR – 26.7 pts)
In a wild shootout, Mheineger just edged out Wastingtime02 155.12 to 139.68, though both teams lit up the scoreboard. Kenneth Walker’s 30.9 points and Tee Higgins’ 26.7 led Wastingtime02’s charge, but Mheineger’s Kyren Williams exploded for 36.7 points, with Carnell Tate adding 21.5. Despite the loss, Wastingtime02 showed firepower aplenty, but Mheineger’s RB dominance proved the difference in a high-scoring thriller.

### NickDagan (2-2) 130.66 – 176.02 TheMianimal (2-2)
**Star Performers:** 🧠 Drake Maye (NE QB – 27.16 pts), 🏃 Alvin Kamara (NO RB – 22.8 pts)
TheMianimal’s high-octane offense lit up the scoreboard with 176.02 points, blowing past NickDagan’s 130.66 despite stellar efforts from Drake Maye and Alvin Kamara. Maye’s 27.16 points and Kamara’s 22.8 kept NickDagan competitive, but TheMianimal’s Nico Collins (30.8) and Kyle Monangai (28.0) were just too much. A tough night for NickDagan’s supporting cast, but the QB and RB showed promise in an otherwise one-sided affair.

### leftovername (2-2) 121.58 – 144.2 warkha (2-2)
**Star Performers:** 🏃 Jonathan Taylor (IND RB – 22.7 pts), 🧠 Lamar Jackson (BAL QB – 18.88 pts)
Warkha’s balanced attack pushed them past leftovername 144.2 to 121.58, fueled by Javonte Williams’ monster 31.3 points and Chuba Hubbard’s 25.9. Leftovername got solid contributions from Jonathan Taylor (22.7) and Lamar Jackson (18.88), but it wasn’t enough to contain warkha’s rushing duo. A close contest that swung on Williams’ big day, leaving leftovername wishing for a bit more from their supporting cast.
