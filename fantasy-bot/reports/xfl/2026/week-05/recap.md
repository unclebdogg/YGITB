# Recap – Week 5

> Window: 12 Oct – 18 Oct

## Warren and Nacua Shine Bright as Naveb Stays Undefeated, Jacross2000 Snags Nail-Biter

**Moments That Mattered**
- Jaylen Warren explodes for 36.8 points, powering naveb to a fifth straight win.
- Puka Nacua’s 37.7 points nearly carry jacross2000 to a comeback over JamesHanlin.
- Christian Watson and Davante Adams combine for over 50 points in mavbenson’s dominant showing.
- Isaiah Likely and Bucky Irving light up the scoreboard for Dell2620’s big win.
- Drake Maye’s 39.46 points lead NickDagan past a Josh Allen-powered Mheineger.

### naveb (5-0) 128.26 – 108.42 RedCordial (2-3)
**Star Performers:** 🏃 Jaylen Warren (PIT RB – 36.8 pts), 🧲 Brock Bowers (LV TE – 23.0 pts)
naveb is cruising at 5-0, thanks largely to Jaylen Warren’s monster 36.8-point outburst on the ground and Brock Bowers’ surprising 23 points as a tight end. RedCordial’s Parker Washington put up a strong fight with 30.4 points, and James Cook chipped in nicely, but it wasn’t enough to overcome the balanced firepower from naveb’s stars. Warren’s rushing dominance really set the tone, while Bowers provided a valuable edge in the tight end slot, helping keep naveb’s undefeated streak alive.

### jacross2000 (2-3) 139.18 – 135.5 JamesHanlin (0-5)
**Star Performers:** 🎯 Puka Nacua (LAR WR – 37.7 pts), 🧠 Jordan Love (GB QB – 25.28 pts)
This one was a thriller with jacross2000 edging out JamesHanlin by just 3.68 points. Puka Nacua was the star of the show with a massive 37.7 points, hauling in catches and making big plays. Jordan Love’s solid 25.28 points at QB helped seal the deal. JamesHanlin fought hard with Malik Nabers and Tyler Warren both lighting up the scoreboard, but it just wasn’t quite enough to overcome jacross2000’s late surge.

### mavbenson (4-1) 137.6 – 100.56 Wastingtime02 (1-4)
**Star Performers:** 🎯 Christian Watson (GB WR – 32.3 pts), 🎯 Davante Adams (LAR WR – 22.1 pts)
mavbenson flexed a powerful WR combo with Christian Watson and Davante Adams combining for over 54 points. Watson’s 32.3 points were a highlight reel in themselves, while Adams provided steady support. Wastingtime02’s Jayden Daniels threw for 32.26 points, but the rest of the lineup just couldn’t keep pace. This dominant WR duo helped mavbenson maintain their strong 4-1 record with relative ease.

### Dell2620 (3-2) 154.62 – 110.34 warkha (2-3)
**Star Performers:** 🧲 Isaiah Likely (NYG TE – 32.4 pts), 🏃 Bucky Irving (TB RB – 31.5 pts)
Dell2620 put on a clinic with Isaiah Likely and Bucky Irving each racking up over 30 points. Likely’s 32.4 points as a tight end was a fantasy dream, while Irving’s 31.5 on the ground and through the air made him a matchup nightmare. Despite Jared Goff’s near 29 points for warkha, the gap was just too wide. Dell2620’s balanced attack and big plays earned them a decisive victory.

### NickDagan (3-2) 143.96 – 108.1 Mheineger (3-2)
**Star Performers:** 🧠 Drake Maye (NE QB – 39.46 pts), 🏃 Jahmyr Gibbs (DET RB – 37.7 pts)
NickDagan’s Drake Maye had a breakout week with a massive 39.46 points, lighting up the scoreboard with both passing and rushing. Jahmyr Gibbs added 37.7 points, dominating on the ground to back up Maye’s heroics. Mheineger’s Josh Allen put up a strong 31.5 points, but it wasn’t enough to keep pace with NickDagan’s duo. This big day catapulted NickDagan to a solid win and a winning record.

### TheMianimal (3-2) 105.4 – 63.78 leftovername (2-3)
**Star Performers:** 🎯 George Pickens (DAL WR – 28.0 pts), 🧠 Joe Burrow (CIN QB – 24.6 pts)
TheMianimal’s George Pickens and Joe Burrow combined for over 50 points, providing a steady scoring base that overwhelmed leftovername. Pickens’ 28 points on the outside and Burrow’s 24.6 from the pocket were the keys to a comfortable win. leftovername’s Jameson Williams and Jonathan Taylor couldn’t match the output, as the team struggled to get going offensively.
