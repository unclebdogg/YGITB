# Recap – Week 6

> Window: 19 Oct – 25 Oct

## Week 6 Shake-Up: High Flyers and Heartbreakers in You Get in the Bowl!

**Moments That Mattered**
- jacross2000's powerhouse backfield of McCaffrey and Walker couldn't quite outscore K309h's balanced attack.
- naveb pulls off a surprise upset over StatutoryRaitests despite the latter’s solid Kittle and Diggs combo.
- Leviathan35's RB duo crushes mavbenson's WR show, but mavbenson still manages a respectable 102 points.
- nlredfern's lethal WR/TE tandem of Smith-Njigba and Likely outguns Tedwood's Green Bay stars.
- Franc19 rides a monster WR/TE day from Nabers and Pitts to dominate leejsv’s valiant QB and RB effort.

### K309h (3-3) 100.62 – 139.06 jacross2000 (3-3)
**Star Performers:** 🏃 Christian McCaffrey (SF RB – 30.7 pts), 🏃 Kenneth Walker (KC RB – 27.9 pts)
jacross2000's backfield blitz featuring Christian McCaffrey and Kenneth Walker lit up the scoreboard with a combined 58.6 points. Despite this, K309h's James Cook and Kirk Cousins put on a solid show, Cook's 24.3 points keeping them in striking range. The difference? jacross2000's RB dominance was just too much to overcome, turning this into a high-scoring RB showcase where the ground game ruled supreme.

### naveb (2-4) 82.9 – 97.78 StatutoryRaitests (4-2)
**Star Performers:** 🧲 George Kittle (SF TE – 17.9 pts), 🎯 Stefon Diggs (WAS WR – 16.4 pts)
In a matchup where points were tighter than a last-minute field goal, StatutoryRaitests leaned on Kittle and Diggs to keep the scoreboard ticking. But naveb's Jayden Daniels and Chris Olave delivered enough punch to sneak past expectations. It was a gritty battle where every point counted, and while StatutoryRaitests had the flashier stars, naveb's steady contributions proved just enough to snatch the win.

### mavbenson (5-1) 102.42 – 148.66 Leviathan35 (5-1)
**Star Performers:** 🏃 Bhayshul Tuten (JAX RB – 24.7 pts), 🏃 D'Andre Swift (CHI RB – 23 pts)
Leviathan35’s RB tandem of Bhayshul Tuten and D'Andre Swift exploded for nearly 48 points combined, steamrolling mavbenson's impressive WR duo of DK Metcalf and Carnell Tate. Despite mavbenson’s strong 102 points, Leviathan35’s ground game dominance was simply overwhelming, turning this clash of titans into a clear victory for the running backs.

### nlredfern (4-2) 132.28 – 115.14 Tedwood (1-5)
**Star Performers:** 🎯 Jaxon Smith-Njigba (SEA WR – 25.4 pts), 🧲 Isaiah Likely (NYG TE – 23.9 pts)
nlredfern’s dynamic WR/TE combo of Smith-Njigba and Likely put on a clinic, combining for nearly 50 points to outgun Tedwood’s Green Bay duo Matthew Golden and Jordan Love. Despite Tedwood’s strong showing, nlredfern’s explosive receiving corps made the difference, lighting up the scoreboard and securing a confident win.

### Franc19 (2-4) 125.3 – 100.6 leejsv (1-5)
**Star Performers:** 🎯 Malik Nabers (NYG WR – 28.5 pts), 🧲 Kyle Pitts (ATL TE – 23 pts)
Franc19’s wideout Malik Nabers and tight end Kyle Pitts combined for an eye-popping 51.5 points, overwhelming leejsv’s valiant Drake Maye and Chuba Hubbard. The duo’s consistency and big-play ability powered Franc19 to a comfortable win, proving that a strong WR/TE stack can dominate even when the opposing QB and RB put up solid numbers.

**Power Pulse**
- 🏆 RBs were the headline grabbers this week, with McCaffrey, Walker, Tuten, and Swift all posting monster scores.
- • Several games showcased tight battles, but a few WR/TE duos like Smith-Njigba/Likely and Nabers/Pitts made the difference.
