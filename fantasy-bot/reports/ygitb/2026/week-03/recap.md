# Recap – Week 3

> Window: 28 Sep – 04 Oct

## Week 3 Thrillers: Undefeateds Hold Strong as Star Power Shines Bright

**Moments That Mattered**
- K309h edges nlredfern in a nail-biter despite Jaxon Smith-Njigba’s monster 35.36 points.
- Bijan Robinson and Brock Bowers absolutely torch Franc19, pushing mavbenson to a dominant 3-0.
- Jahmyr Gibbs’ 41.4 points carry Leviathan35 to another convincing win over leejsv.
- naveb’s balanced attack and stingy Minnesota defense topple jacross2000, who leaned heavily on Wilson and CMC.
- Tedwood pulls off the upset over StatutoryRaitests with Tyler Shough’s breakout QB performance.

### K309h (3-0) 120.64 – 117.5 nlredfern (1-2)
**Star Performers:** 🎯 Davante Adams (LAR WR – 20.7 pts), 🧠 Lamar Jackson (BAL QB – 20.44 pts)
K309h held on tight in a thriller against nlredfern, who nearly pulled off the upset thanks to a huge 35.36-point explosion from Jaxon Smith-Njigba. But it was the steady hand of Davante Adams and Lamar Jackson that made the difference, each delivering over 20 points to keep K309h undefeated. Despite Christian Watson's strong showing for nlredfern, the margin was razor-thin, making this one of the week's most heart-stopping finishes.

### naveb (1-2) 115.34 – 103.62 jacross2000 (1-2)
**Star Performers:** 🎯 Chris Olave (NO WR – 19.7 pts), 🛡️ Minnesota (MIN DEF – 17.0 pts)
naveb bounced back from a rough start by shutting down jacross2000’s firepower. While Garrett Wilson and Christian McCaffrey put up solid numbers, naveb's Chris Olave led the charge with nearly 20 points, complemented by a stout Minnesota defense that snagged 17 points. This combo proved too much for jacross2000, swinging the momentum back in naveb's favour.

### mavbenson (3-0) 164.74 – 91.7 Franc19 (0-3)
**Star Performers:** 🏃 Bijan Robinson (ATL RB – 35.3 pts), 🧲 Brock Bowers (LV TE – 27.6 pts)
mavbenson smashed through Franc19 with a jaw-dropping 164.74 points, powered by Bijan Robinson's beastly 35.3 points and Brock Bowers’ 27.6 points at tight end. Franc19’s Stafford and Deebo Samuel put up respectable numbers but were left in the dust as mavbenson cruised to a perfect 3-0 start. This was a clinic in offensive dominance.

### Leviathan35 (3-0) 144.28 – 91.16 leejsv (0-3)
**Star Performers:** 🏃 Jahmyr Gibbs (DET RB – 41.4 pts), 🧠 Brock Purdy (SF QB – 31.28 pts)
Leviathan35 is the real deal, storming to 3-0 behind a monster week from Jahmyr Gibbs who racked up 41.4 points — an absolute fantasy clinic. Brock Purdy’s efficient 31.28 points at QB sealed the deal against leejsv, whose top performers Jeremiyah Love and Trey McBride couldn’t keep pace. This one was never really close once Gibbs hit full throttle.

### Tedwood (1-2) 115.9 – 136.68 StatutoryRaitests (2-1)
**Star Performers:** 🧠 Tyler Shough (NO QB – 23.8 pts), 🎯 Matthew Golden (GB WR – 21.0 pts)
Tedwood pulled off a surprise victory over StatutoryRaitests, sparked by Tyler Shough’s breakout 23.8 points and Matthew Golden’s solid 21 points. Despite George Kittle and Ja'Marr Chase posting big numbers for StatutoryRaitests, it wasn’t enough to overcome Tedwood’s balanced attack. This result shakes up the standings and shows the league’s depth.
