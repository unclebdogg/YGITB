# Recap – Week 2

> Window: 21 Sep – 27 Sep

## Week 2 Shakeup: K309h Stays Perfect, Leviathan35 Stumbles in Nail-Biter

**Moments That Mattered**
- K309h's lethal WR duo of Davante Adams and DeVonta Smith put on a clinic, carving up leejsv’s defence for a tight 134.6-125.02 win.
- In a nail-biter down to the wire, naveb edged Leviathan35 by just over a point despite Leviathan35’s Jahmyr Gibbs shining on the ground.
- Jacross2000’s backfield power with Kenneth Walker and Christian McCaffrey overwhelmed StatutoryRaitests, turning the tide despite Ja'Marr Chase’s big day.
- Mavbenson’s Dak Prescott and Jaylen Waddle combo proved too much for nlredfern, even with Jaxon Smith-Njigba lighting up the scoreboard.
- Tedwood leaned on CeeDee Lamb’s explosive day and Brandon Aubrey’s reliable boot to overcome Franc19, who got a strong showing from Jonathan Taylor.

### K309h (2-0) 134.6 – 125.02 leejsv (0-2)
**Star Performers:** 🎯 Davante Adams (LAR WR – 39.5 pts), 🎯 Amon-Ra St. Brown (DET WR – 35.2 pts)
K309h's perfect start continued thanks to Davante Adams' jaw-dropping 39.5 points and DeVonta Smith adding a hefty 27.7. The Rams' star was unstoppable, slicing through leejsv's defence all game. Despite Amon-Ra St. Brown torching coverage with 35.2 points and Trey McBride's solid 18.1, leejsv couldn’t quite close the gap in this high-scoring affair. K309h’s balanced WR assault proved the difference in a match that could have swung either way.

### naveb (0-2) 88.04 – 89.06 Leviathan35 (2-0)
**Star Performers:** 🎯 Chris Olave (NO WR – 22.6 pts), 🏃 Jahmyr Gibbs (DET RB – 23.3 pts)
This one was a heartbreaker for Leviathan35, coming up just shy by a single point. Jahmyr Gibbs powered his way to 23.3 points, keeping Leviathan35 in the hunt. But Chris Olave’s dependable 22.6 and Jayden Daniels' steady QB play for naveb proved just enough to eke out the win. A tough loss for the undefeated Leviathan35 who’ll want to tighten things up after this close call.

### jacross2000 (1-1) 106.86 – 123.38 StatutoryRaitests (1-1)
**Star Performers:** 🏃 Kenneth Walker (KC RB – 23.8 pts), 🎯 Ja'Marr Chase (CIN WR – 26.5 pts)
StatutoryRaitests cruised to a solid 123.38-106.86 victory behind Ja'Marr Chase’s explosive 26.5 points. Jacross2000 fought valiantly with Kenneth Walker and Christian McCaffrey combining for over 46 points, but it wasn’t enough to contain the big-play potential of Chase. Omarion Hampton’s contribution was notable but fell short of turning the tide. StatutoryRaitests gets back on track with this convincing performance.

### mavbenson (2-0) 125.76 – 97.52 nlredfern (1-1)
**Star Performers:** 🧠 Dak Prescott (DAL QB – 29.76 pts), 🎯 Jaxon Smith-Njigba (SEA WR – 42.5 pts)
Mavbenson kept the perfect run alive, powered by Dak Prescott’s near 30-point masterclass and Jaylen Waddle’s 21.8 points. While nlredfern’s Jaxon Smith-Njigba dazzled with a whopping 42.5 points, the rest of the lineup couldn’t match mavbenson’s balanced attack. Justin Jefferson’s quiet 8.5 points was a surprise and left nlredfern struggling to keep pace in this one-sided clash.

### Tedwood (1-1) 117.58 – 90.88 Franc19 (0-2)
**Star Performers:** 🎯 CeeDee Lamb (DAL WR – 35.3 pts), 🏃 Jonathan Taylor (IND RB – 29.2 pts)
Tedwood leaned heavily on CeeDee Lamb’s explosive 35.3 points and Brandon Aubrey’s 17-point kicking to secure a comfortable win over Franc19. Despite Jonathan Taylor’s strong 29.2 points and Matthew Stafford’s near 27 points, Franc19 couldn’t overcome the firepower from the Dallas duo. Lamb’s big day was a key factor in tipping the scales in Tedwood’s favour.
