# Recap – Week 1

> Window: 14 Sep – 20 Sep

## Week 1 Blitz: Jeanty’s Ground Game & Caleb Williams’ Showtime Ignite the Season

**Moments That Mattered**
- Ashton Jeanty bulldozes for 32.7 points, steering K309h to a nail-biter over naveb.
- Kenneth Walker and Zay Flowers torch the scoreboard, powering jacross2000’s dominant debut.
- Caleb Williams dazzles with 37.26 points, lifting nlredfern to a massive 201-point blowout.

### K309h (1-0) 127.96 – 124.26 naveb (0-1)
**Star Performers:** 🏃 Ashton Jeanty (LV RB – 32.7 pts), 🧠 Lamar Jackson (BAL QB – 24.96 pts)
K309h edged out naveb in a thriller thanks to Ashton Jeanty’s bruising 32.7-point performance. Jeanty’s relentless ground game set the tone, while Lamar Jackson added a slick 24.96 points through the air and on the move. Naveb’s Chris Olave dazzled with 28.2 points and Ladd McConkey chipped in 19.2, but it wasn’t quite enough to topple K309h’s balanced assault. This one came down to the wire, with K309h’s duo outpacing naveb by a slim margin to claim the opener.

### jacross2000 (1-0) 156.52 – 77.7 Franc19 (0-1)
**Star Performers:** 🏃 Kenneth Walker (KC RB – 34.1 pts), 🎯 Zay Flowers (BAL WR – 26 pts)
jacross2000 stomped out Franc19’s challenge with a commanding 156.52 to 77.7 win. Kenneth Walker’s explosive 34.1 points spearheaded the charge, while Zay Flowers dazzled with 26 points, lighting up the scoreboard. Despite Jonathan Taylor’s solid 25.1 and Bucky Irving’s handy 20.3, Franc19 never got close to matching jacross2000’s firepower. This was a statement game, setting the tone for jacross2000’s season with a potent RB-WR combo that simply overwhelmed the opposition.

### mavbenson (1-0) 145 – 100.46 Tedwood (0-1)
**Star Performers:** 🏃 Derrick Henry (BAL RB – 35.3 pts), 🏃 Bijan Robinson (ATL RB – 31.3 pts)
mavbenson’s ground game steamrolled Tedwood in a 145 to 100.46 win, led by Derrick Henry’s monstrous 35.3 points and Bijan Robinson’s impressive 31.3. The duo combined for a punishing RB assault that Tedwood struggled to contain. Breece Hall and CeeDee Lamb put in respectable efforts but couldn’t match the sheer volume and consistency of mavbenson’s stars. This matchup showcased the power of dominant running backs setting the pace early in the season.

### Leviathan35 (1-0) 141 – 81.86 StatutoryRaitests (0-1)
**Star Performers:** 🏃 Jahmyr Gibbs (DET RB – 33.6 pts), 🏃 D'Andre Swift (CHI RB – 32.4 pts)
Leviathan35 cruised to a solid 141 to 81.86 victory over StatutoryRaitests thanks to a dynamic RB duo. Jahmyr Gibbs and D'Andre Swift racked up 33.6 and 32.4 points respectively, combining agility and yardage to keep the scoreboard ticking. StatutoryRaitests’ Kyren Williams and Joe Burrow couldn’t quite ignite their side, posting underwhelming returns that left them trailing. ### nlredfern (1-0) 201.36 – 130.52 leejsv (0-1)
**Star Performers:** 🧠 Caleb Williams (CHI QB – 37.26 pts), 🎯 Justin Jefferson (MIN WR – 31.2 pts)
nlredfern absolutely lit up the scoreboard with a monstrous 201.36 points to overwhelm leejsv’s 130.52. Caleb Williams put on a show with 37.26 points, showcasing elite QB skills that kept defenses guessing. Justin Jefferson added a huge 31.2 points to keep the pressure relentless. Despite Amon-Ra St. Brown’s strong 28.7 and Trey McBride’s 24.5, leejsv couldn’t keep pace with this offensive onslaught. A true fantasy clinic from nlredfern’s stars, setting a high-water mark for Week 1.
