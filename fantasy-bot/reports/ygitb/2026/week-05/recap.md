# Recap – Week 5

> Window: 12 Oct – 18 Oct

## Week 5 Shocker: Underdogs Bite Back as Top Dogs Stumble

**Moments That Mattered**
- Jahmyr Gibbs explodes for 37.7 points but Leviathan35 still falls short in a nail-biter.
- Puka Nacua and Jayden Daniels combine for a monster 70 points to lift naveb to a huge upset.
- Jaylen Warren’s 36.8 points keep mavbenson undefeated in a solid win over StatutoryRaitests.

### K309h (3-2) 112.8 – 136.74 Leviathan35 (4-1)
**Star Performers:** 🏃 Jahmyr Gibbs (DET RB – 37.7 pts), 🏃 James Cook (BUF RB – 22.8 pts)
Leviathan35's Jahmyr Gibbs was a beast with 37.7 points, powering his side to a hefty 136.74 total. Trevor Lawrence also chipped in 21.74 points, making for a strong showing. But K309h’s crew wasn’t just rolling over; James Cook rumbled for 22.8 points while Davante Adams caught fire for 22.1. Despite the power from Leviathan35, K309h’s balanced attack kept it closer than expected, though the underdog couldn’t quite pull off the upset.

### naveb (2-3) 145.76 – 157.4 Franc19 (1-4)
**Star Performers:** 🎯 Puka Nacua (LAR WR – 37.7 pts), 🧠 Jayden Daniels (WAS QB – 32.26 pts)
In a high-scoring thriller, Franc19’s Bucky Irving and Josh Allen both dazzled, posting 31.5 and 30.5 points respectively. But naveb’s Puka Nacua was the real showstopper, lighting up the scoreboard with 37.7 points alongside Jayden Daniels’ impressive 32.26. This dynamic duo carried naveb to a massive 145.76 points, eking out a narrow victory against a tough Franc19 side.

### jacross2000 (2-3) 142.2 – 148.54 nlredfern (3-2)
**Star Performers:** 🧲 Isaiah Likely (NYG TE – 32.4 pts), 🎯 Garrett Wilson (NYJ WR – 23.4 pts)
nlredfern edged out jacross2000 in a shootout, thanks largely to Isaiah Likely’s monster 32.4 points and Christian Watson’s 32.3. Jacross2000’s Garrett Wilson and Sam LaPorta put up solid numbers but just fell short. The matchup was tight all game, but nlredfern’s explosive receiving duo made the difference when it counted.

### mavbenson (5-0) 138.14 – 122.6 StatutoryRaitests (3-2)
**Star Performers:** 🏃 Jaylen Warren (PIT RB – 36.8 pts), 🧠 Joe Burrow (CIN QB – 24.6 pts)
Mavbenson remains unbeaten thanks to Jaylen Warren’s explosive 36.8 points and Brock Bowers’ reliable 23.0. StatutoryRaitests’ Joe Burrow and Ja'Marr Chase put forth a strong effort but couldn’t quite close the gap. The steady production from mavbenson’s stars kept them comfortably ahead, preserving their perfect start to the season.

### Tedwood (1-4) 92.74 – 142.76 leejsv (1-4)
**Star Performers:** 🧠 Drake Maye (NE QB – 39.46 pts), 🎯 George Pickens (DAL WR – 28.0 pts)
Despite a monster 39.46 points from rookie QB Drake Maye, leejsv came up short against Tedwood, who leaned heavily on George Pickens’ 28.0 points and Matthew Golden’s 20.3. Leejsv’s Trey McBride added 21.6, but the overall output just couldn’t match Tedwood’s balanced attack. A rare win for Tedwood in a battle of struggling teams.
