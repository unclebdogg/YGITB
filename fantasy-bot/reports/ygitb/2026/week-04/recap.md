- K309h pulls off a gutsy win over undefeated mavbenson despite a monster day from Bijan Robinson.
- naveb edges Tedwood as Puka Nacua and Bryce Young deliver clutch performances against CeeDee Lamb’s blazing 41.3 points.

### K309h (3-1) 111.68 – 153.1 mavbenson (4-0)
**Star Performers:** 🧠 Lamar Jackson (BAL QB – 18.88 pts), 🏃 Ashton Jeanty (LV RB – 18.6 pts)
K309h shocked the league by toppling the unbeaten mavbenson, even though mavbenson’s Bijan Robinson and Tee Higgins went bonkers with 27.7 and 26.7 points respectively. The win was carved out by consistent scoring from Lamar Jackson’s savvy QB play and Ashton Jeanty’s strong running, combining for nearly 38 points. Despite the star power from mavbenson, K309h’s balanced attack and timely plays proved just enough to snap the undefeated streak in a nail-biter.

### naveb (2-2) 124.76 – 110.14 Tedwood (1-3)
**Star Performers:** 🎯 Puka Nacua (LAR WR – 27.7 pts), 🧠 Bryce Young (CAR QB – 21.46 pts)
naveb’s balanced firepower from Puka Nacua and Bryce Young proved the difference against Tedwood, who leaned heavily on a jaw-dropping 41.3 points from CeeDee Lamb. While Lamb’s heroics lit up the scoreboard, naveb’s duo kept the pressure constant, combining for nearly 50 points and outpacing the rest of Tedwood’s squad. Tedwood’s Tyler Shough couldn’t quite match the output needed, handing naveb a hard-fought but deserved victory.

### jacross2000 (2-2) 163.12 – 130.96 leejsv (0-4)

### Leviathan35 (3-1) 74.48 – 119.18 nlredfern (2-2)
**Star Performers:** 🏃 Jahmyr Gibbs (DET RB – 17.7 pts), 🎯 Drake London (ATL WR – 14.6 pts)

However, the stars shone brighter on nlredfern’s side with Javonte Williams lighting it up for 31.3 points and Jared Goff adding 20.48. Leviathan35’s Jahmyr Gibbs and Drake London put up respectable numbers but couldn’t quite lift their team past the potent attack from nlredfern. This game was a strange one, where the scoreboard told a different story than the final result.

### Franc19 (0-4) 119.42 – 154.92 StatutoryRaitests (3-1)
